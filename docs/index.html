A personal learning portfolio

Turning AI research into small, inspectable experiments.

AI Thinking Lab is a place to read research, ask a narrower engineering question, build a reproducible demonstration, and preserve what the evidence actually shows.

AI-assisted work. ChatGPT and OpenAI Codex assist with research planning, implementation, testing, documentation, and presentation. The experiments are specific evidence, not proof of error-free code or general AI capability.

A repeatable way to learn

  1. Read and cite

    Start with a research paper and identify the ideas worth understanding.

  2. Narrow the question

    Turn a broad concept into a practical engineering question.

  3. Build and define

    Use small fixtures, explicit expected behavior, and inspectable code.

  4. Run and preserve

    Test the implementation, record results, and state what remains unknown.

About the work

This portfolio is AI-assisted. I direct the learning goals and use automated tests and recorded traces to inspect each experiment. I do not claim to have personally hand-written AI-generated code or documentation.

Experiment 001 is a deterministic engineering simulation, not a real-LLM evaluation or a reproduction of MemRiskBench. Each experiment keeps its research attribution, methodology, findings, and limitations with the evidence.
